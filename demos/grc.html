GRC Risk Toolkit: what-if risk report

A NIST CSF 2.0 cyber risk assessment, scored in your browser by the same engine as the Python toolkit. Change any control's status, or apply the remediation roadmap, and watch residual risk, the heat map and the risk register update.

Maplewood Credit Union is fictional and all data is made up for demonstration. The browser engine was checked against the Python toolkit on 300 random combinations of control statuses: every score, rating and roadmap matched.

Remediation roadmap

Every open control gap is simulated as implemented and ranked by the residual risk it removes per unit of effort (S = 1, M = 2, L = 4). The ranking recalculates as you change controls.

Control assessment

22 NIST CSF 2.0 subcategories with the evidence behind each status. Preventive controls (Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect) cut likelihood by up to 60%; response and recovery controls and backups cut impact by up to 40%.
