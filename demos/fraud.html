What analysts see: the alert queue

Ten alerts sampled evenly from Gradient Boosting's top 132 (the 0.5% budget). Every alert carries plain-language reason codes, so an analyst knows where to look first.

Why the evaluation is built this way

Train on the past, test on the future

A random split lets a model peek at the future and inflates results. Here the models learn from earlier weeks and are scored on two weeks they never saw.

Point-in-time features

Every feature uses only transactions before the current one. A test proves that adding future data never changes an earlier feature.

Budget and dollars, not accuracy

With 0.29% fraud, a model that flags nothing is 99.7% accurate. What matters is how many fraud dollars sit in the alerts a team can actually review.
